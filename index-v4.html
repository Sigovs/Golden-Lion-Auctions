    How the hammer falls

    No one wins a lot by bidding in the last second. A bid in the final two minutes puts the clock back to two minutes, and the lot closes only when two full minutes pass without a bid.

    This is the rule Bring a Trailer uses. Golden Lion's own rules, fees and auction length are not decided yet.
