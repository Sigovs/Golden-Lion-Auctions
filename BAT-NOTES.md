# What we take from Bring a Trailer

Read live on bringatrailer.com, 6 Oct 2026 (home, /auctions/, /how-bat-works/). BaT is the
functional benchmark (BRIEF decision 2). This file is what it actually does, so HOME and the
SRP copy its mechanics, not our memory of them. Fees and rules below are **BaT's**; Golden
Lion's own are not decided and must not be printed as if they were.

## The home page, top to bottom

1. **Header:** logo, one wide search ("Search for make, model, category, or anything else…"),
   Auctions · Submit a Vehicle · How BaT Works · language · Watchlist (star) · Log In.
   Second row: Shipping, Makes and Models, Categories, Locations, Get Alerts, Verified
   Checkout, Local Partners, Stories, Event Calendar, Podcast, About.
2. **"New to BaT? Learn how it works."**: a one-line onboarding bar.
3. **Featured Auctions**: a carousel of large cards: photo left, headline, country chip,
   a paragraph of the car's story, then `Bid: USD $250,000 | ⏱ 3 days`.
4. **Latest Bids (1,252 auctions now live)**: a live ticker table: title · current bid ·
   time left (clock in red under a day, `16:47:18`), next to a photo of the hovered row.
   **"No Reserve:" is a prefix in the title itself.**
5. **The feed**: newest listings as editorial posts: date — Category / Premium | N Comments,
   big headline, photo, story excerpt.
6. **Sidebar**: The BaT Daily email signup, features, upcoming events, categories, origin.

## The auctions page (the SRP)

- "Current Auctions · 1,252 Auctions Now Live · Go to My Watchlist".
- Grid / list toggle, a search field, **Sort by: Ending Soonest** (default).
- Filters: Location (postal code), Listing Type, Category, Era, Origin, Year Range,
  **Ending within**, Current Bid, Exclude.
- Card: photo · **headline title** · country flag · badges (**No Reserve**, **Premium**,
  **Alumni** = the car sold on BaT before) · two lines of story · `Bid: USD $20,000 | ⏱ 13:53:05`.

## The single most useful thing: titles are headlines

BaT never titles a lot "2001 Honda S2000". It writes **"One-Family-Owned, 3,600-Mile 2001
Honda S2000"**, "Ex–Carroll Shelby 1967 Shelby Mustang GT500 4-Speed", "Original-Owner…",
"12k-Mile…", "No Reserve: …". The provenance *is* the title. For Golden Lion, whose whole
difference is the documented record, this is the native format, and it must come from Patton's
feed or the inspection, never be invented (CP1).

## Rules of the room (BaT's, quoted)

- Auctions typically run **7 days**.
- **Register to bid**; every bid is backed by a **credit-card hold** for the buyer's fee.
- **Sniping protection**: "extends the auction clock by two minutes each time a bid is placed
  in the auction's final moments. The auction isn't over until two full minutes have elapsed
  since the highest bid."
- Every listing has a **comment thread**; sellers answer in it; "Contact Seller" for viewings.
- Sellers may **lower the reserve** while live.
- **Reserve not met** → seller and high bidder get **24 hours** to exchange offer/counteroffer.
- Results distinguish **Sold for $X** from **Bid to $X** (reserve not met).
- Fees: seller $99 listing (+$330 Plus photography; White Glove on request). Buyer **5% or
  10%** by category, min $250, cap $7,500 (5%) / $4,000 (10%).
- **Verified Checkout**: title transfer, payment and shipping online.

## What this means for HOME

| BaT has | Golden Lion HOME should |
|---|---|
| Featured carousel | A lead lot that can be stepped through (01 → 12), not a frozen hero |
| Latest Bids ticker with the live count | A live closing board: count, lot, bid, clock, red/urgent under a day |
| Headline titles with provenance | Headline titles from feed facts only (mileage, owner history) |
| No Reserve / Premium / Alumni badges | No Reserve · Ending · Verified are the only badges we can back |
| Comments count on every lot | A comment count slot (sample) on cards |
| Sort: Ending Soonest + filters | Sort + Make / Era / No reserve on HOME; the full set on the SRP (stage 2) |
| Sniping protection explained | "How the hammer falls": the two-minute rule, **marked as proposed** |
| Sold for / Bid to | Results that say which, not just a price |
| BaT Daily email | A dispatch signup in the footer |
