How the hammer falls

Every lot closes on a two-minute window. A bid inside it refills the window, so the car goes to whoever is still bidding when the room goes quiet.

  1. 0102:00–00:30Bidding openClosing window · 02:00Final two minutes

    The last two minutes

    The lot enters its final two minutes. Bidding is open; the current bid is $410,000 (sample).

  2. 0200:07→02:00ExtendedClosing window · 02:00Extended · back to 02:00

    A late bid

    A bid of $415,000 lands with seven seconds left. The lot doesn’t close: the clock goes back to two minutes, so nobody wins by bidding in the last second.

  3. 0302:00–00:00Going once · twiceClosing window · 02:00No new bid

    Two quiet minutes

    Nobody outbids. Two full minutes pass with no new bid. (Time compressed here.)

  4. 0400:00SOLDSold for · sample $415,000 · Bidder 14Closing window · 02:00Closed

    The hammer

    Reserve met: the lot goes to the last bidder at $415,000 (sample).
