How the hammer falls

Any bid placed in the final two minutes resets the clock to two minutes. Nobody wins by waiting for the last second: the car goes to whoever is still bidding when the room goes quiet.

  1. 010:09

    The last two minutes

    The clock runs down; bidding stays open.

  2. 022:00

    A late bid

    Any bid in the final two minutes puts the clock back to 2:00.

  3. 030:00

    Two quiet minutes

    The lot closes only after two full minutes with no new bid. Here the time is compressed.

  4. 040:00SOLD

    The hammer

    Reserve met: sold to the highest bidder.
