## What we take (the principle, not the picture)

**1. `vault/semlerpremium-dk-showroom` (3, in): the SRP.**
Semler solves *"a premium site must not stop working as inventory"* by keeping the
SRP structure as it is and stripping out the dealer noise. The photo takes most of
the card, the data sits as a quiet layer underneath, and there is one restrained
action. **For us** that means the lot card is the car on the stage, then
year/make/model, then **one data line: current bid · time left**, in tabular
figures. Status (Live / Ending / Sold) is the only accent on the card, so that is
where the gold goes. No badges, no Place Bid on the card, only watch.

**2. Semler again: every car in the same dark studio.**
84 cars read as one collection because they were all shot in one setting, with the
same light and the same floor. **For us** that means the generated stage isn't
decoration, it's **a system**. All 12 lots stand in one place, and that's what turns
a "dealer" into a "house". It's also the honest answer to the client's "on stand".
Semler's recorded weakness: big cards magnify weak photos. Patton's feed is uniform
(a white showroom), so the cut-out will come out even. Check resolution in the pilot.

**3. `vault/semlerpremium-dk-brands-porsche-911-gt3…` (3, in): the VDP.**
Semler solves *"show the data without killing desire"* by giving the data a
hierarchy instead of hiding it. Under the car sits one bar of 4 values (price, km,
location, action). The page runs desire → inspection → story → highlights → data →
related. **For the HOME hero** that means one bar under the car: **current bid · time
left · bids · reserve**, plus "View lot". For the lot page later, the same
sequence, with the Golden Lion Verified report in the place of "highlights".

**4. `vault/rolls-roycemotorcars-com-en-us-home-html` (3, in): one environment.**
Rolls solves *"the interface feels pasted on top of the film"* by having the video,
the background and the UI read as one continuous space, not as a rectangle on a
page. **For us** the stage doesn't end at the photo's edge: its floor and darkness
**are** the ground of the page, and the car stands in the page rather than in a
frame. Its recorded weakness: text over frosted glass loses legibility, so data
over the stage goes on a solid tone and gets measured.

## What we avoid

**5. `vault/rmsothebys-com` (1, OUT): the only auction house in the vault, and an anti-reference.**
"Exceptional automotive assets trapped inside a generic corporate-luxury CMS." After
the hero the page falls apart into card grids, mechanical 50/50 bands and arbitrary
light/dark alternation. "VIEW LOT" is an underlined link, not a button. **For us**
that means:
- after the hero the page holds its dominant and subordinate (the feed of lots is the second mass, not "yet another grid");
- no zebra of bands;
- an action is always a button.

The one thing worth keeping: the lot text sits in the lower left as one block in
three registers (title / line / date), not centred over the car.
